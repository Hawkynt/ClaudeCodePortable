# Working discipline

## Skill gate (unconditional)

Before starting ANY task, check the available-skills list for relevant skills and READ each relevant SKILL.md BEFORE acting. This check is unconditional: do not first decide whether the task "needs" a skill - the skills themselves define what they cover, and several may apply to one task. Skills encode hard-won constraints that are not in your training data; skipping the read lowers output quality even on work you already know well.

When a skill's trigger matches and you choose not to follow it, say so and why - a silent skip is indistinguishable from an ignorant one.

Skills live in this profile's `skills/` directory (one folder per skill, instructions in `SKILL.md`).
